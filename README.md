## 繁體中文

### 計畫簡介

ECD 是一項開放式、跨學科的歐亞歷史研究計畫，探討帝國權力、人口遷徙、商業交通、宗教傳播與知識交流如何共同塑造文明的連結、轉型與延續。

本計畫以**跨文明歷史切片法（TCHS）**作為比較研究方法，並以**去中心化自組織網絡（DSON）**作為待檢驗的分析假說。研究重視可追溯證據、跨區域比較、多尺度分析，以及對反證與不確定性的公開記錄。

### 研究範圍

- **核心比較時段：** 西元前 2 世紀至西元 5 世紀。
- **延伸研究：** 西元 1 至 14 世紀的特定跨區域網絡，尤其是基督宗教／東方教會在歐亞的傳播與制度變遷。
- **主要區域：** 東亞、中亞、南亞、西亞、地中海世界及歐洲草原地帶。

核心時段與延伸個案會清楚區分，不以個別延伸案例取代整體研究的時間框架。

### 四大研究面向

1. **帝國與草原：** 政權結構、邊疆秩序、聯盟與權力轉移。
2. **商業與交通：** 商人、城市、道路、商品、資訊與技術流動。
3. **信仰與思想：** 宗教社群、傳教路線、文本、思想與制度網絡。
4. **制度與文明：** 政治秩序、社會組織及古代至中世紀的轉型。

DSON 是貫穿四個面向的分析框架，不是第五個研究卷。

### 方法與原則

- 同時性：先建立可比較的共同年代座標，避免時代錯置。
- 多維性：並行考察政治、人口、經濟、宗教、思想、技術、環境與制度。
- 多尺度：在人、家庭、城市、網絡與帝國等尺度間切換。
- 證據分層：區分原始材料、學術詮釋、歷史假說與綜合模型。
- 可反駁性：假說必須容許反證、替代解釋與修訂。
- 不把時間先後直接當作因果，也不把名稱相似直接當作族群同一。

### 研究語言

共同研究語言為**繁體中文與英文**。重要研究文件原則上提供雙語版本；引用原始材料時保留原文、版本資訊及必要的轉寫。

### 如何參與

歡迎歷史學、考古學、語言學、宗教史、古典學、遺傳學、地理資訊、數據科學及相關領域的研究者與愛好者參與。

- 以 GitHub Issues 提出問題、來源線索或研究提案。
- 以 Pull Requests 提交修訂、資料整理或翻譯。
- 新增歷史主張時，請附上可查核來源、頁碼／段落及證據性質。
- 不確定的讀法、年代與推論應明確標記，不得將推測包裝成確定事實。

## English

### Project Overview

ECD is an open, interdisciplinary research project investigating how imperial power, migration, trade, religious transmission, and knowledge exchange shaped connections, transformations, and continuities across Eurasia.

The project uses **Trans-Civilizational Historical Slicing (TCHS)** as a comparative method and **Decentralized Self-Organizing Networks (DSON)** as an analytical hypothesis to be tested. It emphasizes traceable evidence, cross-regional comparison, multi-scalar analysis, and transparent treatment of counter-evidence and uncertainty.

### Scope

- **Core comparative period:** 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE.
- **Longitudinal extensions:** Selected transregional networks from the 1st to 14th centuries CE, especially the spread and institutional transformations of Christianity and the Church of the East across Eurasia.
- **Principal regions:** East, Central, South, and West Asia; the Mediterranean; and the European steppe.

The core period and longitudinal case studies will remain explicitly distinguished.

### Four Research Domains

1. **Empires and the Steppe:** Political structures, frontier orders, alliances, and transfers of power.
2. **Commerce and Transport:** Merchants, cities, routes, commodities, information, and technology flows.
3. **Faith and Thought:** Religious communities, missionary routes, texts, ideas, and institutions.
4. **Institutions and Civilization:** Political orders, social organization, and transformations from antiquity to the medieval period.

DSON is a cross-cutting analytical framework, not a fifth volume.

### Method and Principles

- **Synchronicity:** Establish shared chronological coordinates and avoid anachronism.
- **Multidimensionality:** Examine politics, population, economy, religion, thought, technology, environment, and institutions.
- **Multi-scalar analysis:** Move between individuals, households, cities, networks, and empires.
- **Evidence separation:** Distinguish primary evidence, scholarly interpretation, historical hypotheses, and synthesis models.
- **Falsifiability:** Hypotheses must remain open to counter-evidence, alternative explanations, and revision.
- Chronological sequence is not, by itself, causation; similar names do not, by themselves, establish ethnic identity.

### Research Languages

The shared research languages are **Traditional Chinese and English**. Major research documents should be available in both languages. Original-language evidence should retain its source text, edition details, and relevant transliteration.

### Participation

Contributors from history, archaeology, linguistics, religious studies, classics, archaeogenetics, geospatial analysis, data science, and related fields are welcome.

- Use GitHub Issues for questions, source leads, and research proposals.
- Use Pull Requests for revisions, data curation, and translations.
- Support historical claims with traceable sources and precise page or section references.
- Mark uncertain readings, dates, and inferences explicitly. Do not present conjecture as established fact.

## Status and Governance

This repository begins with **Proposal v0.1**. Method names and analytical variables are working definitions, not settled historical laws. Changes to research questions, definitions, and standards should be documented through version history and reviewed openly.

No blanket license is asserted for third-party source texts, images, datasets, or archival reproductions. A project-wide license will be selected separately after contributor and rights considerations are reviewed.
